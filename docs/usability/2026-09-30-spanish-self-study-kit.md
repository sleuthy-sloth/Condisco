# Spanish independent-practice self-study kit

Prepared 2026-09-30. Status: study not started. This is a solo usefulness check, not a proficiency test or evidence about other learners. You supply the responses and observations; the AI coder can prepare fixtures and fix defects but cannot complete the study on your behalf.

## Schedule

Day 0: baseline tasks below, before looking at models or transcripts. Record date/build, completion, assistance, and points conveyed. Days 1–6: 10–15 minutes daily across listening, open writing and dialogue in Spanish units 18–26. Day 7 or later: use the parallel tasks below before revealing models. Record the actual elapsed days; do not backdate a delayed retest.

## Baseline form A

1. Listening decision: open `es-b1-trabajo-listen` (“La llamada de la oferta”). Listen once without transcript, identify the offer's important conditions and state which detail would affect your decision. Log replays separately. After answering, inspect the transcript and note which details you missed.
2. Connected writing, no model: write a short Spanish message explaining that you cannot attend a planned Saturday meal, why, and two practical alternatives. Self-check: reason is understandable; alternatives are clear; ideas connect. Save your response outside the app if you want a comparison later; app recordings remain temporary.
3. Spoken response, no model: explain in Spanish that a booked journey has changed, ask for another option, then respond to a follow-up about timing. Use a B1 hosted dialogue when possible. Log whether you could answer before opening its model.

## Retest form B — unfamiliar scenario

1. Listening decision: use the repair/problem listening lesson (`es-b1-problema-listen`, “La llamada al servicio técnico”), without transcript. Explain what the problem is and which next action the speakers agree to take. Follow the same replay/transcript recording rule as baseline.
2. Connected writing, no model: write a Spanish message changing a Sunday outing because of weather, explaining the problem and offering two alternatives. Apply the same meaning/clarity/connection checks, without copying the earlier message.
3. Spoken response, no model: explain that a proposed meeting time conflicts with work, ask for another option, and respond when the other person asks whether next week is possible. Keep it spontaneous; log pauses, assistance and whether the other person's question made sense.
4. Delayed recall: choose three phrases saved during days 1–6. Recall each from its meaning or situation before revealing its target text. Record recalled, partial or missed; do not treat a recognized answer after reveal as independent recall.
