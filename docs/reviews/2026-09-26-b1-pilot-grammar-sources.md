## Unit 18 — Viaje interrumpido

### A1 — Preterite/imperfect in narration; había vs hubo
- **Claim:** Preterite foregrounds perfective events; imperfect gives background (states, setting, habit). *Hubo* counts a bounded event; *había* denotes an ongoing state; both tenses intermix freely in narration.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, *Nueva gramática de la lengua española* (NGLE), §23.12–23.13 (opposition *canté / cantaba*). https://www.rae.es/gramática/sintaxis/el-pretérito-imperfecto-cantaba-iii-relevancia-del-modo-de-acción-la-oposición-canté--cantaba
- **Source 2:** RAE, "Español al día" — *«Había muchas personas», «ha habido quejas», «hubo problemas»*. https://www.rae.es/espanol-al-dia/habia-muchas-personas-ha-habido-quejas-hubo-problemas
- **Disposition:** Both accept the foreground/background split; *había* (state) vs *hubo* (event) confirmed.
- **Note:** Different works, same institution (RAE/ASALE); undisputed.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### A2 — Sequence markers (primero, luego, después, al final, entonces, de repente)
- **Claim:** *Luego* and *después* are both ordering connectors; register difference (*luego* somewhat formal, *después* common colloquial). *De repente* is an adverbial of suddenness, not a sequencer.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, *Nueva gramática básica de la lengua española* (NGB), §16.3 "Los conectores discursivos". https://www.rae.es/gramática-básica/la-preposición-la-conjunción-la-interjección/los-conectores-discursivos
- **Source 2:** RAE/ASALE, NGLE §30.13a group 10 "De ordenación" + §30.13s. https://www.rae.es/gramática/sintaxis/conectores-discursivos-adverbiales-ii-clases-semánticas
- **Disposition:** Both list *luego/después/al final/entonces* in the ordering group; register nuance not disputed.
- **Note:** Different works, same institution; undisputed.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### A3 — Conditional politeness (querría ≈ quisiera > quería > me gustaría)
- **Claim:** The politeness spectrum over *querer* forms is as stated; *quería* in service contexts is standard colloquial politeness, not past tense.
- **Source 1:** J. B. Butt & C. Benjamin, *A New Reference Grammar of Modern Spanish*, 6th ed., Routledge, 2018 — conditional/politeness treatment (§21.3.3 area).
- **Source 2:** Lawless Spanish Grammar, "Polite requests: *querría, quería and quisiera*". https://progress.lawlessspanish.com/revision/grammar/querria-queria-and-quisiera-as-forms-of-courtesy
- **Disposition:** Register gradation confirmed; *quería* accepted as colloquial politeness.
- **Note:** Independent pair (different authors, institutions, publishers).
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### A4 — Reported speech: dijo que + tense sequence
- **Claim:** Backshift in *dijo que* is common but not obligatory (present may stay present, preterite may stay preterite); B1 default is *dijo que* + imperfect for ongoing situations.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, NGB §25.5 "Discurso directo y discurso indirecto". https://www.rae.es/gramática-básica/oraciones-subordinadas-sustantivas/discurso-directo-y-discurso-indirecto
- **Source 2:** RAE/ASALE, *Glosario de términos gramaticales*, s.v. "discurso indirecto" (refs. NGLE §43.9–43.10). https://www.rae.es/gtg/discurso-indirecto
- **Disposition:** Tense adaptation described, not mandated; non-backshifted sequences accepted.
- **Note:** Different works, same institution; undisputed.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### A5 — por/para in travel contexts
- **Claim:** *Por* = cause/route/means ("¿por qué?" / "¿por dónde?"); *para* = purpose/destination/deadline ("¿para qué?" / "¿para cuándo?"). Travel application: *por las obras* (cause) vs *para las siete* (deadline).
- **Source 1:** RAE, *Diccionario de la lengua española* (DLE), s.v. *por* (cause, route, means senses). https://dle.rae.es/por
- **Source 2:** RAE/ASALE, *Diccionario panhispánico de dudas* (DPD), s.v. *porque* §1a–b (causal *porque* = *por* + cause; final = *para* + purpose). https://www.rae.es/dpd/porque
- **Disposition:** Cause vs purpose distinction supported; travel context is a direct application.
- **Note:** Different works, same institution; undisputed.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### A6 — Requests/register at a claims desk
- **Claim:** Softened complaint (*quisiera reclamar*, *me gustaría reclamar*) is the conventional formal register; *quería* colloquial politeness also acceptable; direct *quiero/exijo* is emphatic but grammatical.
- **Source 1:** Lawless Spanish Grammar, "Polite requests" + "Indirect Speech". https://lawlessspanish.com/grammar/indirect-speech
- **Source 2:** RAE/ASALE, *Glosario de términos gramaticales*, s.v. "discurso indirecto" (complaint relay = reported speech + polite request). https://www.rae.es/gtg/discurso-indirecto
- **Disposition:** Register spectrum confirmed; no dispute in the literature.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

## Unit 19 — El trabajo y los estudios

### B1 — Futuro simple vs ir a + inf. vs present for plans
- **Claim:** Futuro = decided/scheduled/predicted; *ir a* + inf. = imminent intention (default for plans in speech); present = fixed schedules — a grammaticalized three-way split.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, NGLE §23.14r (futuro) + §28.8c–e (*el verbo ir*, prospective value; rejected in temporal clauses with future). https://www.rae.es/gramática/sintaxis/
- **Source 2:** F. Matte Bon, "Maneras de hablar del futuro en español…", *RedEle* 6 (2006), Ministerio de Educación. https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/dam/jcr:32d4e732-fdfe-4d29-96ae-c113c693bcfe/2006-redele-6-09mattebon-pdf.pdf
- **Disposition:** Teach the tricolon with *ir a* preferred for plans in speech; register distinction noted.
- **Note:** Independent pair (academy grammar + journal article).
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### B2 — porque + sentence (pack first use at u18L4/L5, corrected 2026-09-26; Unit 19 practises it in plan reasons)
- **Claim:** *Porque* (one word) is the causal conjunction introducing a full clause; orthography distinguishes it from *por qué* (interrogative) and *por que* (prep. + relative).
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, DPD s.v. "porque" §1–§4. https://www.rae.es/dpd/porque
- **Source 2:** RAE, "Español al día" — *porque / por qué / por que*. https://rae.es/espanol-al-dia/porque-porque-por-que-por-que-0 ; corroborated by FundéuRAE, "porqué, porque, por que y por qué". https://www.fundeu.es/recomendacion/porque-porque-por-que-y-por-que-935/
- **Disposition:** Teach the orthography rule alongside the causal use; canonical connector.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### B3 — deberías vs debes for advice
- **Claim:** *Deberías* (conditional) softens into advice; *debes* (present) states stronger obligation — register/force difference.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, DPD s.v. "deber" §2a–b. https://www.rae.es/dpd/deber
- **Source 2:** FundéuRAE, "deber + infinitivo y deber de + infinitivo, diferencia" (2025-09-19). https://www.fundeu.es/recomendacion/deber-de-infinitivo-obligacion-suposicion/
- **Disposition:** *Deberías* = default advice form; *debes* = firmer obligation.
- **Note:** Different works; FundéuRAE is RAE-affiliated but a separate body with its own editorial recommendations.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### B4 — Comparatives/relatives in work talk
- **Claim:** Inequality: *más/menos… que*; equality: *tan(to)… como*; *que* (not *quien*) links comparatives; relative *que* covers persons and things, *quien* is personal-only.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, NGLE §45.1a–g "Características generales de las construcciones comparativas". https://www.rae.es/gramática/sintaxis/características-generales-de-las-construcciones-comparativas
- **Source 2:** RAE/ASALE, NGB §13.2.1.1–3 "Los relativos *que, quien, cual y cuyo*". https://www.rae.es/gramática-básica/relativos-interrogativos-y-exclamativos/descripción-de-los-relativos/los-relativos-que-quien-cual-y-cuyo
- **Disposition:** Confirm the u16 pattern; *que* covers most relative needs in work talk.
- **Note:** Different works, same institution; undisputed. (The two sources cover the two halves of the claim: comparatives, relatives.)
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### B5 — Present subjunctive light revisit (espero que + subj)
- **Claim:** *Esperar que* + present subjunctive is the canonical trigger; the subjunctive is the primary pattern (future indicative possible but marked).
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, NGLE §25.5g (alternation contexts in substantive clauses). https://www.rae.es/gramática/sintaxis/el-modo-en-las-subordinadas-sustantivas-iii-otros-contextos-de-alternancia
- **Source 2:** RAE, *Libro de estilo de la lengua española*, "El modo: ¿indicativo o subjuntivo?" §5.1. https://www.rae.es/libro-estilo-lengua-española/el-modo-indicativo-o-subjuntivo
- **Disposition:** Light revisit defensible; *espero que* is the minimal productive trigger for B1.
- **Note:** Different works, same institution; undisputed.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### B6 — estar de + activity vs ser + role  *(amended 2026-09-26)*
- **Claim:** *Ser* + role-noun asserts stable professional/identity category (*es médico*); *estar de* + role asserts temporary situational occupancy (*está de camarero en Inglaterra* = working as a waiter, time-bound).
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, DPD s.v. "estar" §3 ("Estuvo de director en el instituto") + s.v. "ser" §2.1 ("Su padre es médico") — **counted as ONE reference** (same work).
- **Source 2 (independent):** J. B. Butt & C. Benjamin, *A New Reference Grammar of Modern Spanish*, §29.2.1 (ser: equational identity, "Es médico") + §29.3.2 (*estar de*: "temporary employment or situation", "Está de camarero en Inglaterra"). Verified live against full text.
- **Disposition:** Cross-institution pair (RAE/ASALE vs King's College London/Routledge); identity vs temporary occupancy confirmed by both.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

## Unit 20 — Una decisión social

### C1 — Gustar-type verbs + porque
- **Claim:** With postposed subject the verb agrees with the thing (*me gustan los museos*), the dative clitic is mandatory; *¿Te gustaría?* / *les encanta* are standard dative invitation forms.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, DPD s.v. "gustar" (intransitive; experiencer as indirect object). https://www.rae.es/dpd/gustar
- **Source 2:** RAE/ASALE, NGLE §35.8v (alternance, argumental indirect object vs subject). https://www.rae.es/gramática/sintaxis/alternancias-del-complemento-indirecto-con-el-sujeto-y-el-complemento-directo
- **Disposition:** Mandatory dative + agreement confirmed.
- **Note:** Different works, same institution; undisputed.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### C2 — porque / por qué / por que orthography  *(source 2 corrected 2026-09-26)*
- **Claim:** Three-way distinction: *porque* = causal conjunction (one word); *por qué* = interrogative/exclamatory (with tilde); *por que* = preposition *por* + relative *que* (test: an article can be inserted — *el motivo por el que*).
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, DPD s.v. "porque" §1, §2, §4. https://www.rae.es/dpd/porque
- **Source 2 (replaces the mis-cited NGB §10.4.2):** FundéuRAE, "«porqué», «porque», «por que» y «por qué»" — covers all four spellings with tests. https://www.fundeu.es/recomendacion/porque-porque-por-que-y-por-que-935/ — **verified live 2026-09-26 (HTTP 200).**
- **Disposition:** DPD definitive; Fundéu recommendation independently corroborates each spelling case.
- **Note:** Different works; FundéuRAE is RAE-affiliated but a separate body. Pair is different-work; undisputed.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### C3 — Invitation formulas (¿te gustaría…?, ¿te apetecería…?, claro que sí, lo siento, no puedo)
- **Claim:** All forms are standard Spanish accept/decline/invitation formulas; *¿te gustaría…?* is the polite conditional invitation.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, DPD s.v. "gustar" (polite/transitive use, indirect-object experiencer). https://www.rae.es/dpd/gustar
- **Source 2:** RAE/ASALE, NGLE §35.8v (gustar alternance; conditional derivable from the pattern). https://www.rae.es/gramática/sintaxis/alternancias-del-complemento-indirecto-con-el-sujeto-y-el-complemento-directo
- **Honest gap (labeled, not closed by a padded citation):** no standalone DPD usage entry exists for *apetecer*, and the **register/frequency ordering** of the set is pedagogical-textbook convention, not a disputed linguistic claim. The forms themselves are standard; ledger row should carry this note.
- **Flag:** straightforward (forms); register ranking = pedagogical convention, recorded as such.

### C4 — para + inf (purpose) vs por (cause)  *(source 2 completed 2026-09-26)*
- **Claim:** *Para* + inf. marks purpose/prospective goal (*para regalar*); *por* + noun marks cause/occasion (*por su cumpleaños*) — purpose is prospective, cause retrospective.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, NGLE §46.1d/h (*por* denotes cause; *para* introduces beneficiary/finality) + §46.10n–ñ (causal vs final locutions). https://www.rae.es/gramática/sintaxis/introducci%C3%B3n-caracter%C3%ADsticas-generales-de-estas-construcciones
- **Source 2 (completed):** RAE, DLE s.v. *para* §1 — "Denota el fin o término a que se encamina una acción". https://dle.rae.es/para — **verified live 2026-09-26 (HTTP 200).**
- **Disposition:** Purpose (DLE sense 1) vs cause (NGLE §46.1d) supported by a dictionary entry and a grammar treatise — different works.
- **Note:** Same institution, different works; undisputed. (Earlier NOT FOUND for DPD *para* stands: the DPD has no stable standalone entry for the preposition.)
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### C5 — Planning frames: vamos a + inf., quedamos en + place, ¿qué te parece?  *(restructured 2026-09-26)*
- **Claim:** Three proposal frames: *vamos a* + inf. (intention), *quedamos en* + place/time (agreement), *¿qué te parece?* (opinion-seeking).
- **Source for frame 1:** RAE/ASALE, NGLE §28.8 "El verbo *ir*" — *ir a* acquires prospective value for plans (treatment verified live by the Unit 19 research, B1 source 1).
- **Source for frame 2:** RAE/ASALE, DPD s.v. "quedar" §1c — "Cuando significa 'acordar'… con un complemento encabezado por *en*: *Quedamos en que…*". https://www.rae.es/dpd/quedar
- **Source for frame 3:** RAE/ASALE, DPD s.v. "parecer" §2 — with indirect object, *parecer* denotes opinion. https://www.rae.es/dpd/parecer
- **Disposition:** One source per frame; two distinct works (NGLE grammar + DPD usage dictionary). The earlier "DPD + DPD" same-work note is resolved by the NGLE source covering frame 1.
- **Note:** Same institution, different works; undisputed.
- **Flag:** straightforward.

### C6 — Clitic combinations: te lo / te la; le lo → se lo (se-rule)
- **Claim:** Order: *se* (3rd-person dative before another clitic) > 2nd > 1st > 3rd; *le lo → se lo* obligatory; \**le lo*, \**me se* normatively rejected.
- **Source 1:** RAE/ASALE, NGLE §16.11 "Secuencias de pronombres átonos" (conditions A–E). https://www.rae.es/gramática/sintaxis/secuencias-de-pronombres-átonos
- **Source 2:** RAE/ASALE, NGB §10.4.2 "Grupos de pronombres átonos" (accessible summary of the same hierarchy). https://www.rae.es/gramática-básica/el-pronombre-personal/colocación-de-los-pronombres-átonos/grupos-de-pronombres-átonos
- **Corroboration (third):** RAE/ASALE, DPD s.v. "Pronombres personales átonos" §4 (order table; rejects \**me se*). https://www.rae.es/dpd/pronombres%20personales%20átonos
- **Disposition:** Three distinct works confirm the identical rule; no dispute in the literature.
- **Flag:** straightforward.
